Set up in a minute.

1

Tap

Hold the top of your iPhone to the sticker, then tap the banner that appears. Mivo sets it up with you.

2

Stick

Peel it off and press it on a clean, flat surface where you reach for your phone: desk, nightstand, car.

3

Tap again

Tap to start your Spot, tap again to end it. A Spot adds a pause. It never locks you out.

Works with iPhone XS or later on iOS 17. Not for bare metal. Every Mivo Spot is unique and connects to one Mivo account. No Mivo yet? The tap opens a page that links to the App Store. Help: usemivo.app/support
